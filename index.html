Introduction

Company Overview

Salim's Rahman's Super Sparks Special Effects (M) Sdn Bhd is a Private company incorporated on 8 November 2000, registered at the Registrar of Companies, Malaysia. With an Authorized Share Capital of RM 1,000,000.00 and Paid Up Capital of RM 1,000,000.00, we have rapidly grown into a well-respected Special Effects and Specialized Service Event Company.

Our Experience

The experience of our in-house team covers award-winning feature films, commercials, music videos, short films, live events, and new media content. Servicing both our international and local client base, we are all about building long-term relationships. Our production pipeline is constantly being reshaped and tailored to support productions.

Global Reach

Salim's Rahman's Super Sparks Special Effects continues to be a strong service provider of movie Special Effects, Explosives, Pyrotechniques, Aerial Display Fireworks, Musical Fireworks, and Specialised Movie Fire Arms services to companies and studios around the world. We have been involved in projects across South Korea, Australia, South Africa, Hong Kong, Thailand, and major events shown everywhere from television to cinema.

02

Company Write Up

We are a premier licensed company specializing in firearms supplies for government bodies, movie production companies, special effects, and equipment supplies for movies, pyrotechnics, fireworks displays for national events, and both government and private event management.

With over a decade of experience, Salim's Rahman's Super Sparks Special Effects (M) Sdn Bhd serves as a consultant and execution works for both local and international companies for movies, and event management. With our well-managed professional and highly trained personnel, safety, discipline, effectiveness, and time are absolute must-have aspects that we give precedence to.

"We provide top quality products and services for our clients at competitive prices. We are your one-stop centre specialised for anything and everything pertinent to our business portfolio."

We have a salutary relationship with several governmental and well-established companies, whereby our years of experience in this sector make it easier for us to get business opportunities. The storage of more than 9,000 movie firearms in Hong Kong includes sniper rifles, shotguns, auto and semi pistols, revolvers, machine guns, and even 15th-century guns, World War II guns, and many more that have played a role in films or television.

We also supply guns and weapons props for movie usage, with associates for firearms suppliers in the United Kingdom, South Africa, Australia, and Thailand. All firearms for movie usage are blank firing, deactivated, or replicas, brought into the country under strict binding Firearms Law. Salim's Rahman's Super Sparks Special Effects (M) Sdn Bhd is a licensed firearms company under the Firearms Act.

All deactivated weapons that have been converted can no longer discharge any shot, bullet, or other missile — they are blank firing guns intended for movie usage, handled by our professional Armourer and well-trained personnel. We offer a range of blank firing weapons as well as deactivated and replica firearms, creating a multitude of interiors as well as exterior sets to meet the needs of most films.
